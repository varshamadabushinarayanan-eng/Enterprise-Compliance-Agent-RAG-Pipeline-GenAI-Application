# MOCK SOP: Data Integrity and Record Review
Document ID: MOCK-SOP-DI-001 | Version: 1.0 | Status: Training example

This document is fictional training content and is not an approved procedure.
## Purpose
Describe a sample approach for reviewing laboratory records for completeness and traceability.
## Review checks
1. Confirm each record has a unique identifier, date, operator attribution, and applicable instrument identifier.
2. Check that entries are legible, attributable, contemporaneous, original or verified true copies, and accurate.
3. Investigate unexplained gaps, overwritten values, missing audit-trail context, or inconsistent timestamps.
4. Record review outcomes and route identified issues through the site's approved quality process.
## Evidence
Retain the reviewed record reference, reviewer identity, review date, observations, and documented disposition.
