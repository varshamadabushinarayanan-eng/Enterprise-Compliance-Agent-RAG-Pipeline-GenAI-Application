# MOCK POLICY: Change Control
Document ID: MOCK-POL-CC-003 | Version: 1.0 | Status: Training example

This document is fictional training content and is not an approved policy.
## Change assessment
A proposed change should have a unique change-control reference, rationale, scope, owner, and planned implementation date.
Assess potential impact on product quality, validated systems, procedures, training, data integrity, suppliers, and regulatory commitments.
## Approval and implementation
Obtain documented cross-functional review and approval before implementation where required by the governing quality system.
Define verification or validation evidence, required document updates, training, and implementation controls.
## Closure
Close the change only after required actions and evidence are reviewed and recorded.
