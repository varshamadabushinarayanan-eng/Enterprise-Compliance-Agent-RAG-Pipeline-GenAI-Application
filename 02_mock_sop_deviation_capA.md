# MOCK SOP: Deviation and CAPA Workflow
Document ID: MOCK-SOP-QA-002 | Version: 1.0 | Status: Training example

This document is fictional training content and is not an approved procedure.
## Purpose
Provide a simplified example of documenting a deviation and corrective/preventive action.
## Workflow
1. Record the event promptly with date, process, product or study reference, and reporter.
2. Describe facts separately from assumptions; preserve relevant raw data and records.
3. Assess potential impact and determine whether containment is needed under approved procedures.
4. Perform a proportionate root-cause investigation using documented evidence.
5. Define corrective actions to address the identified cause and preventive actions where justified.
6. Assign owners and due dates; document effectiveness checks and closure approval.
## Required fields
Deviation ID, event description, immediate action, impact assessment, investigation evidence, root cause, action owner, due date, effectiveness result, and approver.
